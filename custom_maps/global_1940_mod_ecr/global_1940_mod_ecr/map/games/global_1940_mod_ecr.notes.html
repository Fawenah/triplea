Global 1940 MOD ECR v3.0 — first implementation

Based on Young Grasshoppers Tournament Edition 1.0.4. Original map and engine credits belong to their respective creators, including Young Grasshopper and Veqryn. This is a separate local variant.

Dice and units

Combat uses d10: hit on the printed value or less. AAA and facility fire hit on 1, cruiser defensive AA has up to 2 shots, and kamikaze hits on 3. Bombing damage and convoy disruption remain d6. Strategic bombing adds 2. Every convoy die counts, with losses capped by the affected territories.

Infantry 3 IPC / A2 D2 M1; artillery 4 / A3 D3 M1; mechanized infantry 4 / A2 D2 M2; tank 6 / A5 D5 M2; anti-tank gun 4 / A2 D3 M1; mechanized anti-tank gun 5 / A3 D5 M2; AAA 3 / no combat / M1; truck 2 / no combat / M2.

Fighter 10 / A4 D5 M4; tactical bomber 10 / A5 D3 M4; strategic bomber 10 / A7 D1 M6; reconnaissance 5 / no combat / M6. Reconnaissance has no special ability and there is no Fog of War.

Submarine 6 / A4 D2; destroyer 8 / A4 D4; cruiser 12 / A5 D6; carrier 16 / A1 D3; battleship 20 / A7 D7; transport 7 / no combat. All ships move 2, with operative base bonuses retained.

Combined arms

Each defending tank or artillery boosts up to 2 infantry from D2 to D3; each infantry receives this bonus only once. Artillery boosts 1 attacking mech infantry to A3. Mechanized infantry requires tank pairing to blitz. Infantry attack and tactical bomber attack do not receive the old combined-arms bonuses. One fresh artillery can accompany one mechanized infantry for up to two spaces in noncombat when both begin and finish together.

Aircraft transport and upgrades

Transport aircraft are free reclassifications of strategic or heavy bombers; cargo aircraft require heavy bombers. They cannot be bought directly. Until conversion enforcement is added, use Edit mode to remove the original bomber and add exactly one corresponding aircraft owned by the same power, in the same territory. Both forms have M6 and no combat value. Transport aircraft carry up to 2 infantry; cargo aircraft carry 1 transportable ground unit. Both forms airlift in noncombat only. Aircraft cargo types and capacity are checked by the engine.

Upgraded unit types exist for manual replacement after an agreed technology discovery: self propelled artillery, heavy tank, jet fighter, heavy bomber, super submarine, super carrier, super battleship, improved transport, and radar units/facilities. Upgrades cannot be bought directly. Research phases are disabled pending the final implementation stage; agree research using d6 and apply upgrades manually. UK Europe and UK Pacific do not share discoveries.

Map, economy, and victory

The YG initial forces, diplomacy, neutral armies, modified turn order, and unplaced-purchase retention are preserved. Siberia is 2 IPC, Soviet Far East 0, Argentina is pro-Axis, South Africa has a victory city, and Algeria connects directly to French West Africa. India upgrades after war is declared at a politics checkpoint. Lend-lease is placed after UK Europe mobilization while the USA is neutral.

National objectives follow the supplied VictoryObjectives reference. Victory tokens persist once earned. Occupation requires Poland, Finland, Bulgaria, and Iraq, excluding Argentina. The winner is decided after 8 rounds; ties go to the Allies. The London convoy alternative uses actual capped losses from original UK Europe territories in the same turn. Tokens are shown in the Objectives tab.

Movement and placement

Railroad movement is enforced: fresh infantry starting Noncombat Move at a major factory may move two spaces, up to the origin territory's IPC allowance. Truck transport does not spend or extend that allowance. Improved transports carry at most two ground units plus one infantry (three units total).

Land stacking is enforced at 10 + territory IPC per side, with allied units counted together. AAA, radar AAA, trucks, factories/bases and attacking aircraft are exempt. Sea zones have no limit. Excess stacks block movement phase completion: move units out or resolve necessary removals in Edit mode. Germany starts with 17 counted units against a limit of 15; its starting forces are preserved, so move at least two counted units out in its first Combat Move.

Combat and remaining rules

Anti-tank hits now take tanks, mechanized infantry and mechanized anti-tank guns first; excess hits are assigned normally. Mechanized anti-tank priority applies only in round one. Strategic bombers on both sides withdraw from ongoing combat after round one. Withdrawn defending bombers are destroyed if enemy ground units capture their territory; they survive when the territory is held or an air-only attack wins without capturing it. Bomber-only attacks on undefended transports roll one d10 per bomber, hitting on 7 or less.

Transport/cargo aircraft automatically revert to bombers/heavy bombers at the end of Noncombat Move. Free conversion before/during movement still uses Edit mode. Supplied mine and reconnaissance images replace the placeholders.

Ordinary fighter interception follows normal AAA: both sides roll before either side removes aircraft casualties, fighters at 1 and jets at 3 on d10. Survivors still fire normally. Before any battle dice, the attacker can select tactical bombers and assign each a single unit type to target at fixed 4 throughout the battle. Excess hits are lost; escorted transports cannot be chosen, and submarines require a destroyer. Unassigned bombers and AI use normal attack 5.

Capital-ship bounties are automated: 1 IPC per sunk battleship or carrier, including upgrades, paid at the active turn's end. The combat participant first in turn order receives the bounty; mine and kamikaze sinks credit their owner.

At the start of Purchase, each eligible destroyer zone offers a mine for 2 IPC: select its destroyer to lay the mine, or None to skip. One mine per allied side per zone, and no more owned mines than owned destroyers. Enemy ships entering or passing through roll one d10 per mined zone per nation per movement phase, hitting on 2 or less. A hit consumes the mine and applies one hit to any moving ship; sunk transports lose cargo. Misses and existing mines after destroyer losses persist. Mine rolls prevent movement undo. AI ships trigger mines; AI mine purchasing and route planning remain deferred.

Detailed paratrooper support/casualty restrictions; naval screening/blockades; ELO and faction bid separation; research progression. Mines cost 2 IPC and are removed on a hit. Factories/bases do not count toward stacking. Capital-ship bounties are paid at end turn. Existing YG behavior applies until the relevant rule is automated.

Free aircraft conversion happens before/during movement using Edit mode and reverses automatically at the end of Noncombat Move. The capital-ship bounty belongs to the participating player first in turn order. Mines roll once per entry/movement phase rather than per ship; existing mines remain after destroyer losses, but new placement is unavailable while over allowance. Both sides' AA shots in ordinary fighter interception fire simultaneously.
