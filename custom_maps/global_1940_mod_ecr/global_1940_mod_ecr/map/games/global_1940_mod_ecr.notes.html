Global 1940 MOD ECR v3.0 — first implementation

Based on Young Grasshoppers Tournament Edition 1.0.4. Original map and engine credits belong to their respective creators, including Young Grasshopper and Veqryn. This is a separate local variant.

Dice and units

Combat uses d10: hit on the printed value or less. AAA and facility fire hit on 1, cruiser defensive AA has up to 2 shots, and kamikaze hits on 3. Bombing damage and convoy disruption remain d6. Strategic bombing adds 2. Every convoy die counts, with losses capped by the affected territories.

Infantry 3 IPC / A2 D2 M1; artillery 4 / A3 D3 M1; mechanized infantry 4 / A2 D2 M2; tank 6 / A5 D5 M2; anti-tank gun 4 / A2 D3 M1; mechanized anti-tank gun 5 / A3 D5 M2; AAA 3 / no combat / M1; truck 2 / no combat / M2.

Fighter 10 / A4 D5 M4; tactical bomber 10 / A5 D3 M4; strategic bomber 10 / A7 D1 M6; reconnaissance 5 / no combat / M6. Reconnaissance has no special ability and there is no Fog of War.

Submarine 6 / A4 D2; destroyer 8 / A4 D4; cruiser 12 / A5 D6; carrier 16 / A1 D3; battleship 20 / A7 D7; transport 7 / no combat. All ships move 2, with operative base bonuses retained.

Combined arms

Each defending tank or artillery boosts up to 2 infantry from D2 to D3; each infantry receives this bonus only once. Artillery boosts 1 attacking mech infantry to A3. Mechanized infantry requires tank pairing to blitz. Infantry attack and tactical bomber attack do not receive the old combined-arms bonuses. One fresh artillery can accompany one mechanized infantry for up to two spaces in noncombat when both begin and finish together.

Aircraft transport and upgrades

Transport aircraft are free reclassifications of strategic or heavy bombers; cargo aircraft require heavy bombers. They cannot be bought directly. Until conversion enforcement is added, use Edit mode to remove the original bomber and add exactly one corresponding aircraft owned by the same power, in the same territory. Both forms have M6 and no combat value. Transport aircraft carry up to 2 infantry; cargo aircraft carry 1 transportable ground unit. Both forms airlift in noncombat only. Aircraft cargo types and capacity are checked by the engine.

Upgraded unit types exist for manual replacement after an agreed technology discovery: self propelled artillery, heavy tank, jet fighter, heavy bomber, super submarine, super carrier, super battleship, improved transport, and radar units/facilities. Upgrades cannot be bought directly. Research phases are disabled pending the final implementation stage; agree research using d6 and apply upgrades manually. UK Europe and UK Pacific do not share discoveries.

Map, economy, and victory

The YG initial forces, diplomacy, neutral armies, modified turn order, and unplaced-purchase retention are preserved. Siberia is 2 IPC, Soviet Far East 0, Argentina is pro-Axis, South Africa has a victory city, and Algeria connects directly to French West Africa. India upgrades after war is declared at a politics checkpoint. Lend-lease is placed after UK Europe mobilization while the USA is neutral.

National objectives follow the supplied VictoryObjectives reference. Victory tokens persist once earned. Occupation requires Poland, Finland, Bulgaria, and Iraq, excluding Argentina. The winner is decided after 8 rounds; ties go to the Allies. The London convoy alternative uses actual capped losses from original UK Europe territories in the same turn. Tokens are shown in the Objectives tab.

Rules still requiring manual handling

Anti-tank casualty priority; tactical bomber category selection; general fighter interception shots; first-round-only strategic bombers and their rolls against undefended transports; detailed paratrooper support/casualty restrictions; railroad movement; territory stacking; capital-ship bounties; sea mines; naval screening/blockades; ELO and faction bid separation; research progression. Mines cost 2 IPC and are removed on a hit. Factories/bases do not count toward stacking. Capital-ship bounties are paid at end turn. Existing YG behavior applies until the relevant rule is automated.
